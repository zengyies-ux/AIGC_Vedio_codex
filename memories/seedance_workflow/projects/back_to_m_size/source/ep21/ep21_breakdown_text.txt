“Fine. Just wait.”
She stood, grabbed her purse, and walked away.
Her heels struck the gallery floor harder with every step.
I watched her leave.
I wasn’t nervous.
I wasn’t afraid.
I only remembered one thought.
The person left behind eventually learned how to get ahead.
Nathan came back and glanced in the direction Sophie had disappeared.
“Everything okay?”
“Yeah.”
“Ready to go? There’s a good sushi place nearby.”
“Let’s go.”——区间：19.5-25s

That night, when I got home, I deleted Sophie and Luke from my contacts.
I didn’t block them.
I deleted them.
Blocking meant I still cared enough to keep a wall between us.
Deleting meant I didn’t need them anymore.
Emma saw my expression on the couch.
“What happened?”
“I finally said everything I needed to say to the people from my past.”
“And then?”——区间：39.5-45s

I leaned against the entryway and looked at the warm lights in my apartment.
At the sunflowers I had bought for myself sitting on the coffee table.
I smiled.
“There is no then.”——结尾
