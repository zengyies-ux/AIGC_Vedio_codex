Chapter 17
The day Luke left, he sent me a very long message.
I didn’t open it.
I simply scrolled past.
I only caught the first few words.
“Sarah, ever since high school…”——台词区间：8-10s
Enough.
I didn’t want to hear another story that started with ever since high school.
June brought one of London’s rare stretches of sunshine.
The first phase of my project wrapped up, and the client’s response exceeded expectations.
During our review meeting, my Director announced that the contract value for phase two had doubled.
I would still lead the team.
Afterward, my coworkers congratulated me.
Someone joked, “Sarah, at this rate, are you going to open a whole new branch over here?”
“Maybe.”——时间区间：33.5-38s
Emma grinned like a proud mother.
“I knew you could do it.”——台词区间：40-41.5s
That night, we opened a bottle of wine and sat on the balcony.
“Do you regret coming to London?” ——Emma asked.
“You tell me.”
“No.”
“Exactly.”
“Have you thought about what happens next? Stay here? Go back?”——时间区间：可以忽略“Emma asked.” 44-53s
I looked toward the Thames in the distance.
“I don’t know. I’m focusing on what’s in front of me.”
“And what about your Luke?”
“My Luke?”
“Don’t pretend.”——时间区间：55-61s
Emma tapped her glass against mine.
“I saw him through the peephole that night. He’s pretty good-looking.”
“What good does that do?”
“Fair.”——时间区间：63-69s
She shrugged.
“If he’s not good to you, then he’s not good enough.”——时间区间：70-72.5
I smiled and finished my wine.
At the beginning of July, Nathan officially confirmed his interest in investing in our company.
During the first round of due diligence, he met with my Director.
When he came back, he said, “Your boss thinks very highly of you.”
“Really?”
“He said the company had been stuck in this area before you arrived. You’re the one who finally made the model work.”——时间区间：87-94.5s
I didn’t respond.
Nathan looked at me.
“Sarah, do you know what your problem is?”
“What?”
“You still don’t really believe you deserve recognition.”——95.5-结尾！
