Chapter 16
It was Luke.
“Sarah, I looked into what Sophie did with your client.”
“Okay.”
“She was wrong, but…”台词区间：0.5s-6.5s
I waited for the but.
“She told me she was desperate to fix things between you. She knew you weren’t reading her messages, so she was trying to find another way to reach you.”——台词区间：7.5-14.5s
I stood in the rain as the wind pushed my umbrella sideways.
“So calling my client was her way of reaching me?”
“I know it sounds wrong—”
“Then we agree.”
“Sarah, can you give her another chance?”
“I gave her eight years of chances.”——台词区间：15.5-27s
He went quiet.
A long time has passed.
Then he said, “I’m going home next week.”
“Okay.”
“You really won’t see me before I leave?”——台词区间：30.5-35s
Rain drummed against my umbrella.
I thought for a moment.
“Luke, did you actually come to London for work, or did you come here for me?”——台词区间：38-42.5s
He said nothing.
But this time, his silence wasn’t an unclear signal.
I understood it perfectly.
He had come for me.
He just would never say it outright.
Because he was used to making me guess.
Used to making me take the first step.
Used to making me move closer.
“Then we don’t need to meet,” I said.
“Sarah—”
“When you get home, be good to Sophie.”——56.5-61s
I hung up.
The rain came down harder.
I didn’t run.
I simply walked home beneath my umbrella.
The cuffs of my pants got soaked.
Water seeped into my shoes.
But every step felt solid.
