Chapter 19
Halfway through the exhibition, Sophie kept finding excuses to move closer to Nathan.
“Wow, this one’s so interesting. What do you think, Nathan?”
“This gallery is huge. Do you come to places like this often?”——区间：4.5-12.5s
Nathan answered politely.
But his body remained angled toward me.
I noticed Sophie’s lips tighten.
As we left the gallery, she stopped me.
“Sarah, can we talk privately?”——区间：19.5-22s
Nathan glanced at me.
I nodded.
He walked away to get a phone call.
Sophie pulled me toward a bench in the corridor.
“Sarah, I’ve missed you so much.”——27.5-30.5s
Her voice dropped, her eyes turned red.
“After you left, I finally realized how hard life was without you.”——33-36.5s
I looked at her.
“What was hard about it?”
“It’s just…”——37.5-40s
She lowered her head and twisted her fingers together.
“No one at work hangs out with me anymore. Luke blames me too. He says you left because of me.”
“Didn’t I?”——区间：42-47.5s
Her head snapped up.
“Sarah, you think it was my fault too?”——区间：49-52s
I didn’t answer.
Her tears immediately spilled over.
“I know I wasn’t perfect before, but I really did think of you as my best friend—”
“Sophie.”——54.5-59.5s
I interrupted her.
“Your way of treating me like your best friend was calling me Fatty in front of everyone, imitating me tugging down my clothes, mocking the sound of a chair under me, costing me a deal, and then pretending to be my coworker so you could contact my client.”——60.5s-74.5s
She cried harder.
“I didn’t mean to! I really didn’t know you cared that much…”
“You didn’t know?”
“I thought you wouldn’t get mad—”
“Because I never got mad before, right?”——区间76s——结束
