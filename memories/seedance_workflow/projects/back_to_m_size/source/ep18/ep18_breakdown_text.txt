In mid-July, I arrived at the exhibition and saw someone who had absolutely no reason to be in London.
Sophie.
She stood at the entrance in a floral dress, her hair curled tightly.
Then she saw me.
Her smile froze.
“Sarah?”——区间：36-36.5

Her eyes widened.
“You… How did you get this thin?”——39.5-41.5s

“Oh my God. I barely recognized you!”
“What are you doing here?” I asked.
“Vacation!”——46-52.5s
She hooked her arm through mine as naturally as ever.
“You never answer my messages, so I had to fly over and see you myself.”——54.5-58.5s
Nathan walked up behind me and glanced at Sophie.
“Your friend?”——61-62s
Sophie looked up at him.
Her eyes brightened.
“Hi! I’m Sophie, Sarah’s best friend. And you are?”
“Nathan. Sarah’s classmate.”——64.5-70.5s
Sophie’s smile widened.
“A classmate? Then let’s all look around together.”——72-75s
She naturally slipped between Nathan and me.
I looked at her completely shameless face and took a slow breath.
It was the same scene I had known for eight years.
Sophie arrived.
And suddenly, all the oxygen in the room belonged to her.
Except this time was different.
“Sophie,” I said. “Do you have Nathan’s contact information?”
“Huh? Not yet—”
“Then maybe another time.”——90-96.5s
I walked around to Nathan’s other side.
“We’re going in.”——98-99.5s
Sophie’s smile stiffened.
Only for a second.
Then it returned.
She followed us into the gallery.
But I could feel her eyes on my back.
Testing.
Displeased.
I didn’t turn around.
